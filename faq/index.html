FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If the answer you need is not here, call (844) 791-SNOW and ask. It is quicker.

Are you insured and licensed?
Yes, we are fully licensed and insured to provide snow and ice removal services. Our insurance coverage includes liability protection to ensure peace of mind for our clients.
Do you offer contracts or one-time services?
Yes, we provide both contract-based and one-time snow and ice removal services tailored to your needs. Contracts offer regular maintenance throughout the season, while one-time services are available for occasional needs.
How do you determine pricing for your services?
Pricing is based on factors such as property size, frequency of service, the complexity of the job, and specific client requirements. We provide transparent pricing and can offer customized quotes upon evaluation of your property.
Will your services disrupt our business operations?
We strive to minimize disruption to your operations as much as possible. Our team works efficiently and can often schedule services during off-peak hours to reduce impact on your business.
What equipment do you use for snow and ice removal?
We utilize a variety of equipment including plows, snow blowers, salt spreaders, and shovels. Our fleet is well-maintained and suited to handle various types of snow and ice removal jobs.
How quickly can you respond to a snow or ice removal request?
We prioritize prompt service during weather events. Response times vary based on factors like the severity of the weather and existing client demands. However, we strive to address urgent requests as swiftly as possible.
What sets your snow removal services apart from other companies?
At Alpine Snow and Ice Management, we prioritize customer satisfaction and safety above all else. Our experienced and skilled team uses the latest technology and equipment to provide efficient and effective snow removal services. We also offer flexible service plans to fit your unique needs and budget.
How do I know when I need professional snow and ice removal services?
It is best to consider professional services when snow and ice accumulation pose safety risks or hinder business operations. Signs include heavy snowfall, icy patches, or when your current resources are inadequate to manage the situation.
What measures do you take to ensure safety during snow and ice removal?
Safety is our top priority. Our team undergoes regular training on safe snow and ice removal practices. We also employ proper signage, use cautionary measures, and adhere to industry safety standards to minimize risks.
Do you offer any additional services apart from snow and ice removal?
Yes, we may offer additional services such as salting, de-icing, sidewalk clearing, and snow relocation. We can discuss your specific needs and tailor our services accordingly.
