Why it matters

Why invest in professional de-icing and anti-icing treatments

  • Prevent accidents

    Reducing the risk of slips, trips, and falls on your property is crucial for the safety of everyone and minimizes potential liability issues.

  • Operational efficiency

    Keeping your pathways, driveways, and parking lots clear of ice ensures your business or property remains accessible and operational, even during severe winter weather.

  • Cost-effective

    Proactively managing ice formation can reduce the need for more extensive snow and ice removal efforts, saving you time and resources in the long run.

  • Environmental responsibility

    We use eco-friendly de-icing and anti-icing products that minimize harm to the environment, including your property landscaping and nearby waterways.

The difference between de-icing and anti-icing

  • De-icing

    This process involves applying materials to ice and snow already present on surfaces to melt them. De-icing is reactive, addressing ice after it has formed to restore safe conditions.

  • Anti-icing

    Anti-icing is a proactive measure, where materials are applied before a snowfall or ice event to prevent the bond of ice and snow to the surface. This treatment makes subsequent snow and ice removal more efficient and reduces the risk of slip and fall accidents.
